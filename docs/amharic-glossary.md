# Amharic glossary

One Amharic word for each idea, used the same way in the staff panel, in messages to members (SMS, push, inbox) and in messages to staff. New Amharic text follows this page; when a word here sounds wrong to people at the gym, change it here first, then everywhere it is used.

## Style

- **Buttons** are short, informal commands: አክል, አስቀምጥ, ሰርዝ, አስገባ.
- **Explanations, help text, empty pages and errors** are polite: ይምረጡ, ያክሉ, ይጠብቁ.
- **Messages to members and staff** are polite too, addressing the reader with -ዎ (አባልነትዎ).
- **Spelling.** Use the common modern forms: ሰ (ሰራተኛ, ስራ, ስራ አስኪያጅ), not ሠ/ሥ; ውጪ; እሁድ; ዓመት; መልዕክት.
- **Plurals** are real Amharic plurals, never an English "s":
  - irregular ones as they are spoken: አባል → አባላት, ሰራተኛ → ሰራተኞች, ቀን → ቀናት, ወር → ወራት, ሰዓት → ሰዓታት, ዓመት → ዓመታት;
  - the rest with -ዎች / -ኦች as they sound: ቅርንጫፍ → ቅርንጫፎች, ክፍያ → ክፍያዎች, ጥቅል → ጥቅሎች;
  - after a number, days, months and visits stay singular (7 ቀን, 3 ወር, 10 ጉብኝት); people take the plural (5 አባላት).
- **No lone prefixes.** ከ, ለ, በ, የ are never a label on their own; a label is a whole word or phrase (ከቀን, እስከ ቀን, የተሸጠበት ቅርንጫፍ).
- **Column headers** are nouns, not verbs: የሚጀምርበት ቀን, not ይጀምራል.
- **Kept as they are:** QR, CSV, Excel, Google Sheets, the gym's own names (plans, branches, announcements), and codes such as NEWYEAR or BOLE.
- **Written in Ge'ez:** ቻፓ (Chapa), ቴሌብር (telebirr), ሲቢኢ ብር (CBE Birr), ኤስኤምኤስ (SMS).

## Dates

Ethiopian date first, with the Gregorian date beside it.

| Where | Format | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Sentences, messages, details | Ethiopian day month year, Gregorian in brackets | ኅዳር 18 ቀን 2019 ዓ.ም. (ኖቬምበር 27, 2026) |
| Tables and short spaces | Ethiopian short, Gregorian numbers | ኅዳር 18, 2019 (27/11/2026) |
| Charts (one label a month) | Ethiopian month and year | ኅዳር 2019 |
| Times | 24-hour, unchanged | 18:00–02:00 |

**Ethiopian months:** መስከረም, ጥቅምት, ኅዳር, ታኅሣሥ, ጥር, የካቲት, መጋቢት, ሚያዝያ, ግንቦት, ሰኔ, ሐምሌ, ነሐሴ, ጳጉሜን.

**Gregorian months, always in full:** ጃንዋሪ, ፌብሩዋሪ, ማርች, ኤፕሪል, ሜይ, ጁን, ጁላይ, ኦገስት, ሴፕቴምበር, ኦክቶበር, ኖቬምበር, ዲሴምበር.

**Weekdays:** ሰኞ, ማክሰኞ, ረቡዕ, ሐሙስ, ዓርብ, ቅዳሜ, እሁድ. **Morning / afternoon:** ጠዋት, ከሰዓት.
